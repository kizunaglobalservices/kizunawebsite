Publishing Collection

From First Manuscript to Global Author Presence

Every author's journey is unique — but the need for professional credibility, global reach, and expert guidance remains the same. The KIZUNA Black & White Publishing Collection was carefully designed to support authors at every stage of their publishing path, whether you are releasing your very first book or expanding your professional authority through multiple formats and enhanced visibility.

Our tiered system — Genesis, Ascend, and Pinnacle — provides a clear progression of value, allowing authors to choose the level of support, speed, and market positioning that aligns with their goals. Each package delivers industry-standard production, worldwide availability, and long-term scalability while ensuring you retain full ownership and creative control of your work.

Through our structured publishing process, your book is professionally formatted, registered, and distributed across major global retail channels, including Amazon and Barnes & Noble, while also being indexed through major discovery systems such as Google. Titles are officially registered with the Books in Print database and cataloged with the Library of Congress, establishing legitimacy and professional recognition in the publishing industry.

What makes KIZUNA different is not only the technical production — but the partnership. We combine publishing expertise with guided support so authors can confidently navigate what is often a complex and overwhelming process. From formatting and cover development to distribution setup and post-launch assistance, our goal is to remove barriers while elevating your book to market-ready standards.

Genesis

Where Authors Begin

The Genesis Package is designed for new and emerging authors who want a strong, credible foundation. It provides essential production services, professional formatting, global distribution, and baseline visibility tools needed to successfully publish a book without unnecessary complexity. This level focuses on confidence-building, accessibility, and legitimacy — making it ideal for first-time authors, personal projects, and initial releases.

The Express Lane to Professional Publishing

The Ascend Package is built for authors ready to move faster and higher. With expanded format options, professional custom cover design, priority production timelines, and additional author copies, this level offers a more elevated publishing experience. It is especially suited for entrepreneurs, speakers, coaches, and professionals who want to establish authority quickly while maintaining premium quality and credibility.

Pinnacle

Maximum Impact and Market Positioning

The Pinnacle Package represents the highest level of support within our Black & White collection. Designed for authors who want the strongest launch advantage, this package combines full production, enhanced marketing integration, priority support, and greater visibility positioning. It is ideal for authors seeking brand growth, thought-leadership credibility, or long-term publishing expansion with a competitive market presence.
